## Bij Afbeelding 1: Snelwerkende Insuline (De B28-B29 Mutatie)
**Titel: Engineering Pharmacokinetics: The Structural Basis of Fast-Acting Insulin**

Jouw SDK simuleert de complexe vertraging (CGM lag & IOB decay) van insuline. Op je poster kun je deze afbeelding tonen om uit te leggen *hoe* de wetenschap die vertraging manipuleert.

**Poster Tekst:**
> "While our SDK models insulin absorption computationally ($t_{max,I}$), this absorption speed is fundamentally governed by protein structure. Modern closed-loop algorithms rely on fast-acting insulin (like Lispro). Structurally, this speed is achieved by swapping just two amino acids on the B-chain: **Proline (B28)** and **Lysine (B29)**. 
> 
> *Highlighted in pink*, this crucial structural mutation prevents the insulin molecules from clumping together into hexamers under the skin, allowing them to enter the bloodstream—and thus the algorithm's control loop—much faster."

## Bij Afbeelding 2: De GLUT4 Transporter (Exercise Mode)
**Titel: Non-Insulin-Mediated Glucose Uptake (NIMGU) via GLUT4**

Deze afbeelding toont het fysieke kanaal waar suiker doorheen stroomt tijdens het sporten in jouw simulatie.

**Poster Tekst:**
> "The IINTS-AF SDK features an advanced exercise physiology model that simulates Non-Insulin-Mediated Glucose Uptake (NIMGU). But what does this mean biologically?
>
> During physical stress, muscles bypass the need for insulin by physically translocating the **GLUT4 transporter** protein to the cell membrane. *Rendered here via AlphaFold*, GLUT4 forms a massive 12-transmembrane barrel, literally acting as a mechanical 'floodgate' that opens to pull glucose out of the blood, independent of the algorithm's insulin delivery."

## Bij Afbeelding 3: De Insuline Receptor
**Titel: The Molecular Target: Insulin Receptor (INSR)**

Deze gigantische structuur laat zien hoe klein insuline eigenlijk is vergeleken met de celwand-receptor.

**Poster Tekst:**
> "Every computational insulin dose proposed by the MPC algorithm ultimately targets this massive extracellular structure: the **Insulin Receptor (INSR)**. 
> 
> By bridging the gap between high-level control engineering and deep structural biology, the IINTS-AF SDK acknowledges that algorithmic decisions in Type 1 Diabetes are not just numbers in a simulation—they are deterministic triggers for complex molecular cascades at the cellular level."
