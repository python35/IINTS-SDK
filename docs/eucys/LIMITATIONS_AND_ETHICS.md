# EUCYS Limitations And Ethics

Use this page as the stable public wording for the limits and ethics scope of the EUCYS-facing story.

The aim is not to weaken the project.
The aim is to make the project scientifically stronger by being exact about what it does and does not prove.

## Core Position

IINTS-AF is a **simulation-first evaluation platform** for AI-guided insulin decision research.

It is:

- a benchmark framework
- a reproducibility tool
- a safety-analysis environment
- a way to compare candidate algorithms against baselines under controlled study conditions

It is **not**:

- a medical device
- a clinical dosing engine
- a replacement for medical supervision
- proof of patient benefit by itself

## What The Project Supports

The project can support claims about:

- reproducible benchmark design
- fair comparison against classical baselines
- safety-on vs safety-off analysis
- subgroup analysis across predefined profiles and scenarios
- calibration and uncertainty reporting when the signals are available
- local and edge-ready execution of the study and demo pipeline

These are meaningful scientific contributions because trustworthy healthcare AI needs better evaluation methods, not only better models.

## What The Project Does Not Support

The project does **not** support claims such as:

- “this system is clinically proven”
- “this system should dose insulin in real patients”
- “this algorithm improves diabetes treatment in the real world”
- “this benchmark replaces clinical trials”

If such claims are made, they go beyond the evidence generated by the SDK.

## Main Scientific Limitations

### 1. Simulation-First Scope

The patient profiles are simulated abstractions.
They are useful for controlled testing, but they are not real human subjects.

So:

- they support preclinical reasoning
- they do not automatically transfer to real-world physiology

### 2. Benchmark Dependence

Results depend on:

- the chosen patient profiles
- the chosen scenario families
- the chosen baselines
- the configured safety layer
- the fixed metrics and seeds

That means the benchmark is rigorous, but still bounded by design choices.

### 3. Dataset Limits

External datasets can strengthen plausibility and realism checks, but they do not turn the platform into a clinical validation study.

They are best described as:

- plausibility references
- realism checks
- external sanity checks

not as proof of efficacy.

### 4. Safety Is Relative To The Encoded Policy

Safety results are always relative to:

- the supervisor logic
- the disturbance models
- the conditions defined in the benchmark

So “safer” in this project means:

> safer under the tested protocol and encoded safety policy

not universally safer in all real-world settings.

### 5. Controller Ranking Can Change

A candidate method may rank differently when:

- the patient population changes
- the scenarios become harsher
- uncertainty signals change
- the feature set changes
- the baselines are expanded

That is normal in honest benchmarking and should be presented as such.

## Ethical Position

The strongest ethical framing is:

> unsafe systems should be filtered out in simulation before real-world use is even considered.

This is why the project emphasizes:

- safety
- transparency
- reproducibility
- explainability

instead of only headline performance.

## How To Talk About The Project Publicly

Good wording:

- “evaluation platform”
- “benchmark framework”
- “simulation-first research tool”
- “safety-first benchmark for insulin-decision research”
- “preclinical testing environment”

Avoid wording like:

- “smart insulin pump ready for patients”
- “AI that treats diabetes”
- “replacement for clinical systems”
- “clinically validated dosing tool”

## Best One-Sentence Limitation Statement

Use this if you need one compact sentence:

> IINTS-AF improves how AI-guided insulin decision systems can be tested and compared in simulation, but it does not by itself establish clinical safety or clinical efficacy in real patients.

## Jury-Proof Ending

If you need a strong closing line for EUCYS:

> The value of this project is not that it claims to solve diabetes care directly, but that it makes AI decision research in diabetes safer, clearer, and more reproducible.
