# Safety Boundary Script

Use this exact tone when presenting hardware, AI, or insulin algorithms.

## Short Version

IINTS is not a medical device and is not used for treatment decisions. It is a research and education SDK for simulation, validation, and reproducible review. Any pump workflow shown here is bench-only and uses fake/test commands, not real insulin delivery.

## Longer Version

The purpose of IINTS is to move risk earlier in the development process. Instead of testing ideas directly on hardware or people, the SDK lets users simulate virtual patients, check data realism, validate outputs, and keep a source-backed evidence trail. That makes discussion with doctors and engineers more concrete, while keeping a strict boundary: no real dosing, no therapy claims, and no clinical deployment.

## Never Say

- "This pump can dose insulin safely."
- "The AI controls diabetes."
- "The simulation proves clinical safety."
- "This replaces doctors."

## Better Say

- "This is a bench demonstration of the software pathway."
- "The algorithm is evaluated in simulation only."
- "The output is evidence for further review, not treatment guidance."
