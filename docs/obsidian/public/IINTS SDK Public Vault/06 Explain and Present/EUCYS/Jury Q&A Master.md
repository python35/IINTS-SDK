# Jury Q&A Master

## Is this a medical device?

No. IINTS is a research and simulation SDK. The pump path is a bench-only demonstrator and does not dose real patients.

## What did you build?

A reproducible SDK for insulin algorithm testing with simulation, data-quality checks, safety supervision, reports, edge workflows, and a locked Pico bench workflow.

## What is the scientific claim?

Insulin algorithm research can be made more reproducible and honest by separating physiology, data quality, safety supervision, and hardware evidence.
